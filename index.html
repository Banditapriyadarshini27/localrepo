Agent-Passport ZSP zero standing privilege · governance middleware
deployed ▸

Standing credentials are a standing risk — a leaked token works forever, even for agents that no longer exist. Agent-Passport ZSP replaces them with just-in-time, tool-scoped, auto-expiring access across a four-agent architecture: Governance Agent, Requestor Agent, MCP Gateway, and Tool Agents.

Anomaly detection flags SQL injection, shell injection, and destructive commands before a token is ever issued, backed by behavioral baselines and rate limiting with auto-revocation. Every action lands in a SHA-256 hash-chain ledger — tamper-proof enough to satisfy EU AI Act and GDPR audit requirements. A live dashboard gives one-click revocation across every agent in a session.
